# Teams

Everyone has a personal workspace, which only they can see. A team is a workspace several people
share: start one with New team, at the foot of the sidebar's list of Doods. Once you are in one,
the sidebar lists every workspace's Doods under its name.

- **Inviting.** Ask people by email address or GitHub username. Each gets a link that works once,
  for seven days. An email address is written to where the deployment can send mail; otherwise
  the link is shown to whoever sent it, to pass on. An invitation to a GitHub username is waiting
  for that account when it signs in. On a deployment where sign-up is by invitation, being
  invited to a team is enough to sign up.
- **Sharing.** Every member can use every Dood in the team, read all of its chats, write in
  them, and make new Doods there. A Dood of your own can be moved into a team, and back.
- **One chat, several people.** A message from someone else has their face and name on it, and
  the Dood is told who is speaking. A message that arrives while the Dood is working joins
  what it is doing; each Dood has a setting to make it wait its turn instead.
- **Who is where.** The list of Doods shows who is with which Dood, a chat shows who is in it
  and who is writing, and the team's page lists everyone who is here.
- **Roles.** Members do all of the above. Admins also invite and remove people, rename the team,
  and move or delete its Doods. The owner also decides who is an admin, and can hand the team
  on or delete it.

What a team shares is everything about its Doods: what a Dood remembers is the team's, a
connector someone signed in to acts as them for whoever is using the Dood, and a computer
connected to a team's Dood runs commands for all of them. Anyone in the team can answer what the
Dood asks leave for, and the others see who did.

A computer belongs either to the person who paired it or to a team. Your own computer is yours
to hand out: only you connect it to Doods, a team's Doods included, and only you answer when
a Dood asks for it. A team's computer can be connected to the team's Doods by anyone in the
team, and anyone in the team can answer for it; whoever paired it, or an admin, can remove it. A
team's Dood can see the team's computers and its members' own, and ask for any of them.
Removing someone from a team ends their access at once, disconnects their own computers from
the team's Doods, and removes the computers they paired for the team, since those run as them.
